# SOUL
What exactly is a SOUL? What constitutes a SOUL? What do different SOULs allow? What nonsensical interpretations of them need to be smashed and thrown into a landfill? Find out in today's glossary of SOULs in Glitchtale! (bundled with Reprise's massive retconning spree)

## Overview
A SOUL is the metaphysical essence of a living entity, manifested physically as a heart-shaped object. It serves as the primary interface between a being and its magical abilities.<br>
The exact properties of a SOUL depend on who or what possesses it.

## Fundamental types

### Human SOUL
A human SOUL is, simply put, a SOUL type that humans specifically possess.<br>
Every human SOUL is composed of a mix of all 7 base traits, and human SOULs generally consolidate into one dominant trait that defines part of their appearance, combat style, and most of their magic arsenal.

Human SOULs can persist for a considerably long time after death, not solely but often due to their innate Determination.

#### Mono-trait SOUL
A mono-trait SOUL is a human SOUL with only one dominant trait. This is by far the most common kind of human SOULs.

#### Poly-trait SOUL
A poly-trait SOUL is likewise one with two or more dominant traits, and it can be developed under rare circumstances.<br>
This greatly enhances the user's magic potential and, for the select few who choose this path, unlocks exclusive compound abilities.

Historically, there have been a handful of naturally born poly-trait SOULs, but the exact number until today remains a mystery.

#### Inverted SOUL
An inverted human SOUL is a human SOUL that possesses at least one inverted dominant trait. It does not matter whether one of more other traits are not explicitly inverted for this classification to apply.<br>
This enhances the user's power even more than having a human SOUL with base traits, but performing an inversion is widely considered magical taboo and ill-advised for its potentially fatal side effects.

### Monster SOUL
A monster SOUL is a SOUL type that monsters specifically possess.<br>
They are upside down compared to human SOULs, and they are purely white on the inside.

Monster SOULs do not possess any of the SOUL traits. Each monster SOUL is deeply attuned to one's body. Because of that, monster magic varies with every user.<br>
Common monster magic ranges from basic elemental magic to mimicking various SOUL traits.

Normal monster's SOULs instantly shatter upon death.<br>
Boss Monsters' SOULs can persist for a while after death, but usually it's too short to make it significant or anything.

## SOUL statuses

### Grey SOUL
A grey SOUL is a human SOUL without a dominant trait.<br>
Every human is born with a grey SOUL, and through magical training, they can naturally develop a specific trait and make it dominant. In rare instances, one can develop multiple traits to create a poly-trait SOUL.<br>
A person can lose their dominant trait(s) often through the act of going against them behaviorally.

On one hand, children are far more susceptible to losing their trait, which will put them back to a grey SOUL. They have just started to develop a personality, so it is to be expected.<br>
They can sometimes develop a completely different trait in the process. Most people generally cycle through at least a handful of traits before eventually settling on one or multiple.

On the other hand, adults are less likely to be able to recover from trait loss. As their dominant trait(s) have been deeply consolidated and they are accustomed to being the way they are, their grey state usually lasts longer and becomes more disruptive.

### Empty SOUL
An empty SOUL is a SOUL without magic.<br>
Humans and monsters alike can have empty SOULs. With an empty SOUL, they can't use any magical abilities because they have drained their magic reserves.

Empty human SOULs retain their dominant trait(s).

One empties their SOUL through exhaustion, magical overexertion, forced extraction, and many more.<br>
Given enough recovery time, the magical capacity can return to empty SOULs.

## The traits themselves

### Base traits
There are 7 base traits, which are Determination, Bravery, Justice, Kindness, Patience, Integrity, and Perseverance.<br>
Every human SOUL naturally contains a mixture of all base traits, with the dominant trait(s) taking the majority.

#### Determination
Determination is the manifestation of... what exactly was it? Willpower? Huh. Isn't that a bit too generic?<br>
It being merely willpower alone is somehow too vague for my standards. Let's say it's willpower AND hope.<br>

It is the rarest naturally occurring trait out of the seven. It is also generally the most powerful trait, but people with Determination aren't completely invincible or undefeatable because they have the world's greatest plot armor ever. They are just really hard to beat.

I have approximately 16 lines worth of retconning I want to write for Determination but I don't have the energy for that now.

Losing Determination is possible as a result of feeling hopeless.

#### Bravery
Bravery is not solely fearlessness. Per canon's words, it can be divided as two different aspects: physical and moral.

Physical bravery makes a person resillient to physical difficulties in general, whether that'd be exhaustion, pain, injuries, and even death. I feel like the latter can be classified as overconfidence depending on the situation. It's rare, but not impossible.<br>
Moral bravery lets one act correctly despite the humiliation, dishonor, or rejection that follows afterward. This isn't saying Justice-coded as much as it used to anymore, so I'll give it a pass.

Losing Bravery is often caused by being filled with cowardice.

#### Justice
Justice is driven by one's act of doing whatever they believe is correct, no matter  the aftermath.

For any action, even if it is objectively wrong, as long as they think it's the right thing to do and it aligns with their worldview, they will do it.

Losing Justice is possible through being unable to fully follow your own moral beliefs, also known as "i can't do this anymore."

#### Kindness
Kindness manifests from the softest and most caring of hearts. That may or may not be an exaggeration.

It is one of the rarest naturally occurring traits, only second to Determination. By that nature, Kindness is also one of the hardest traits to master.<br>
Very few ever go out their way to actually practice Kindness and live by it long enough to make it their dominant trait.

Losing Kindness is possible from acting selfish or be cruel towards others.

#### Patience
Patience develops from what I can summarize as "learn how to wait so long time slows down."

Losing Patience can result from being impatient and rushing.

#### Integrity
Integrity runs off of pure truthfulness. One lie ane it's game over, even if it's for good purpose.

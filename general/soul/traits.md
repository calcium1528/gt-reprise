## The traits themselves

### Base traits
There are 7 base traits, which are Determination, Bravery, Justice, Kindness, Patience, Integrity, and Perseverance.<br>
Every human SOUL naturally contains a mixture of all base traits.

#### Determination
Determination is the manifestation of... what exactly was it? Willpower? Huh. Isn't that a bit too... generic?<br>
It being merely willpower alone is somehow too vague for my standards. Let's say it's willpower AND hope.<br>
It is the rarest naturally occurring trait out of the seven. It is also generally the most powerful trait, but people with Determination aren't completely invincible or undefeatable because they have the world's greatest plot armor ever. They are just really hard to beat.<br>
I have approximately 16 lines worth of retconning I want to write for Determination but I don't have the energy for that now.<br>
Losing Determination is possible as a result of feeling hopeless. That's just canon saying it, not me.

#### Bravery
Bravery, per canon's words, can manifest from two different aspects.<br>
Physical bravery makes a person resillient to physical difficulties in general, whether that'd be exhaustion, pain, injuries, and even death. I feel like the latter can be classified as overconfidence.<br>
Moral bravery lets one act correctly despite the humiliation, dishonor, or rejection that follows afterward. That is desperately screaming Justice-coded to me, but, sure, I guess???<br>
Losing Bravery is possible as a result of  being filled with cowardice.

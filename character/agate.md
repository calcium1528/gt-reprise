The one thing—or person, if you will—I've always wanted to make sense of is Betty.<br>
Who or what is she?<br>
I know this page is supposed to be about Agate and Agate specifically, but in Reprise, Agate IS Betty. The latter is just a new identity.

Canon says Betty inherits Agate's memories, but so far she has not questioned who she was for the entirety of Season 2. Could be a coincidence. Could be she actually doesn't remember. Which is objectively worse.<br>
Betty does recall Agate as her "creator". That means she must have literally forgot her old identity and framed her past self as essentially no more than the person who built her. Actually fine, albeit kind of convoluted. Perhaps the Bête Noire ritual screwed up her memories. Defendable if so.

I made a point on this on the dedicated Bête Noire page, but yes, in a now deleted comic, Betty wondered where her brother was. Well, as of now, no she doesn't, but previouslt as Agate she did. That's Copper.<br>
A minor memory slip-up. That's probably the single bit of confirmation I needed to canonize "Agate = Betty" in Reprise. There's probably a crap ton more but it's the strongest clue, second to the fact that yes who am I kidding Betty has Agate's SOUL.<br>
I just wished it weren't merely a comic.

Now Amber enters the question. She shouldn't even be invited here because her SOUL was already destroyed, and hence I can't ask whether Betty is Amber anymore, but I'm not asking that. Her SOUL is gone. Destroyed by who, you may ask? Her own sister Agate. Truly tragic.<br>
For around 17 million different reasons Agate could have just used herself for both the SOUL AND vessel. The Bête Noire ritual/spell doesn't give a fuck about age! Murdering her own sister is kind of wasteful, if I do say so myself. Absolute strategic throw.<br>
Though that does give me more material to exploit her emotions, potentially for some kind of, what, an alternate route? Maybe, just maybe... a *weird* route.

Continuing on that, yes, I read the whole fucking wiki and realized the Bête Noire ritual has already been performed at least once. That explains why it's forbidden now.<br>
Perhaps Agate going out of her way to learn deep Runic shit might have been a little overkill? It's safe to assume she knew what she was getting herself into. Being salty because you lost to your twin brother in a conflict really does drive you insane.

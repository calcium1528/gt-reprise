The one thing—or person, if you will—I've always wanted to make sense of is Betty.<br>
Who or what is she?<br>

Canon says she inherits Agate's memories, but so far she has not questioned who she was for the entirety of Season 2. Could be a coincidence. Could be she actually doesn't remember. Which is objectively worse.<br>
Betty does recall Agate as her "creator". That means she must have literally forgot her old identity and framed her past self as essentially no more than the person who built her. Actually fine, albeit kind of convoluted. Perhaps the Bête Noire ritual fucked up her memories. Defendable if so.

Now Amber enters the question. She shouldn't even be invited here because her SOUL was already destroyed. Destroyed by who, you may ask? Her own sister Agate. Truly tragic.<br>
For around 17 million different reasons Agate could have just used herself for both the SOUL AND vessel. Murdering her own sister is kind of wasteful, if I do say so myself. Absolute strategic throw.<br>
Though that does give me more material to exploit her emotions, potentially for some kind of, what, an alternate route? Maybe, just maybe... a *weird* route.

## Timeline
- 10XX: The Human Kingdom is founded.
- Late 11XX: Formation of the Wizard Council.
- Early 12XX: Tension arises between the Human Kingdom and Monsterkind.
- Late-mid 12XX: The Human-Monster War.
- Late 12XX: The creation of the Barrier and the Human Kingdom sealing Monsterkind in the Underground.
- Early 13XX: The Lightvale Incident(TM) and the summoning of Bête Noire.
- 13XX-14XX: The original Council members begin leaving their positions.
- Early 14XX: The last original Council member resigns from their position.
- Early-mid 14XX: The Wizard Council is disbanded due to lack of hereditary and willing rulers.
- 198X: The first human, Chara, falls into the Underground.
- 199X-201X: Six more humans follow Chara's steps, but never return alive.
- 201X: Frisk falls into the Underground.
- 201X-205X: Frisk experiments with different outcomes, eventually exhausting every possible scenario. Technically lasting 4 years, the compounding effects of multiple RESETs and time dilation far outstretched this timespan.
- Early January 205X: Season 1's beginning and Frisk's last No Mercy run.
- Early-mid January 205X: The Barrier is broken and Monsterkind is freed.
- Mid Feburary 205X: Season 2's beginning and Betty's first appearance in Determino.
- Late Feburary 205X: Frisk's incomplete erasure from the timeline.
- Early March 205X: Chara's repair of the timeline.
- Mid March 205X: HATE is defeated.
- 206X: Credits.

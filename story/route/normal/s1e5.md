## Plot

### Outside the bubble
Sans is woken up by Papyrus, who is accompanied by Gaster, Undyne, and Asgore. They are all confused by this giant black ball at the Barrier.<br>
Undyne attempts to break it with her spear, but it doesn't leave a scratch. Asgore is able to pierce it with his trident, but even that gets pushed out.

Gaster was able to see a faint black layer of HATE encompassing Chara and Flowey while Flowey was transforming to Asriel. As he did so completed, the walls thickened, trapping both of them inside.<br>
It was too bright to see clearly, but that is the only plausible theory.

Asgore doesn't believe Gaster's words at all, insisting Chara's return is impossible, much less Asriel's. Gaster assures him it will all make sense.

Realizing the bubble is practically impenetrable, they all hope for whoever's inside it the best.

### Inside the bubble
Chara and Asriel stand opposite to each other in the HATE bubble.<br>
Before either of them engage, Chara brings up their situation with Frisk.

First, Chara's history with Frisk has been complicated since they went for their first No Mercy run.<br>
What started as a side-experiment became a recurrence, and only now has Frisk stopped. They RESET every time they took down Sans.<br>
The repeated runs eventually built up HATE in Frisk's SOUL, which soon made its way into Chara. The glitches gave them a new SOUL and a tangible vessel of their own.

Second, Frisk's choice to name the SAVE file after themself started it all.<br>
The so-called "Hard Mode" that Chara says this world is in did not normally let Frisk past Ruins. Nevertheless, they continued, and they were able to unlock interactions that did not exist prior to the renaming.<br>
"Hard Mode", in spite of its literal name, did not stop Frisk from committing countless No Mercy runs, which made the HATE within them build up even faster.

Chara, using their newly acquired Determination, gives Asriel one single choice: ERASE.<br>
They claim it is the only way to properly purge the glitches from this world, and not let them spread and slowly deterioate this timeline.

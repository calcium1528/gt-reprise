## Plot

### Outside the bubble
Sans is woken up by Papyrus, who is accompanied by Gaster, Undyne, and Asgore. They are all confused by this giant black ball at the Barrier.<br>
Undyne attempts to break it with her spear, but it doesn't leave a scratch. Asgore is able to pierce it with his trident, but even that gets pushed out.

Gaster was able to see a faint black layer of HATE encompassing Chara and Flowey while Flowey was transforming to Asriel. As he did so, the walls thickened, trapping both of them inside.<br>
It was too bright to see clearly, but that is the only plausible theory Gaster could come up with.

Asgore doesn't believe Gaster's words at all, insisting Chara's return is impossible, much less Asriel's. Gaster assures him it will all make sense.

Realizing the bubble is practically impenetrable, they all hope for whoever's inside it the best.

### Inside the bubble
Chara and Asriel stand opposite each other in the HATE bubble.<br>
Before either of them engage, Chara brings up their situation with Frisk.

First, Chara's history with Frisk has been complicated since they went for their first No Mercy run.<br>
What started as a side-experiment became a recurrence, and only now has Frisk stopped. They RESET every time they took down Sans.<br>
The repeated runs eventually built up HATE in Frisk's SOUL, which soon made its way into Chara. The glitches gave them a new SOUL and a tangible vessel of their own. They are themself an anomaly that this timeline wouldn't have let exist without Frisk's actions.

Second, Frisk's choice to name the SAVE file after themself started it all.<br>
The so-called "Hard Mode" that Chara says this world is in did not normally let Frisk past Ruins.<br>
Nevertheless, they continued beyond the limits, and they were able to unlock interactions that did not exist prior to the renaming, contributing to the glitches.<br>
"Hard Mode", in spite of its literal name, did not stop Frisk from committing countless No Mercy runs.

Chara, using their newly acquired Determination, gives Asriel one single function, manifesting as a floating button: ERASE.<br>
They claim it is the only proper way to purge the glitches from this world, and not let them spread and slowly deterioate this timeline.

Asriel dismisses Chara's proposal, stating Frisk forgave him, so they deserve a second chance. Chara is not convinced, and they counter with the fact that Frisk did No Mercy AFTER True Pacifist.<br>
That doesn't get Asriel to back down, and he goes as far as to destroying the ERASE button.

### Pacifying the First Fallen (Battle of New Home)
The siblings' beliefs conflict, and Chara is unable to pursue Asriel to do what they wanted. They abandon their timeline erasure plan and decide to fight Asriel.

It's an even match at first. Asriel trades blows with Chara, and none really gets a meaningful opening to exploit.

The HATE surrounding the two becomes an environmental hazard factor. Sometimes it mistakes who the enemy is and targets Chara, which lets Asriel get an upper edge.<br>
That slight advantage is quickly counteracted as Chara reasserts their control over the HATE.

Frisk, unable to physically participate but still present alongside Asriel, advises that he change strategies and resolve the encounter in a different way.<br>
Fighting with a HATE-fueled Chara like this isn't going to do anything, and it will actually tire Asriel out first.

Asriel knows of an accessory the two previously had in common, which should get Chara to recall their past memories.

He abruptly stops and pulls out a golden object hanging on his neck: a Heart Locket.<br>
Chara stutters, slowing down to take theirs out.

Asriel admits he cannot keep fighting Chara, and that violence is not the answer; but he believes that somewhere in there, deep inside, Chara still cares about him, and he wants to change their mind.<br>
Chara denies feeling any remorse and holds their locket up to shatter it, but Asriel can tell the slash felt forced.

Asriel plans to get rid of the HATE that is occupying Chara, but overwhelming firepower alone won't cleanly separate the two.<br>
Frisk suggests using the SOULs that Asriel absorbed, but they don't know how. They try to call for help, but the SOULs won't listen. Asriel takes it into his own matter and uses attacks from all six SOULs.

One by one, the attacks chip away at the HATE, slowly recovering Chara's memories. Asriel reminds Chara of all the things they've done together, and how Chara was still a nice person before all of this.<br>
Chara begins to get more desperate, denying any of that ever happened and repeatedly telling Asriel to stop.<br>
In the end, when Asriel gets to use Determination for the final blast, the HATE bubble explodes.

Asriel loses his God of Hyperdeath form and reverts to his child self, and the majority of the HATE leaves Chara entirely, fleeing the Underground.

### Aftermath of the encounter
Chara stands up feeling lightheaded. Asriel reassures them and embraces them, relieved they are back.

Those previously outside the bubble have mixed reactions, especially Asgore, who can't believe what is happening in front of him right now.<br>
Asgore wants to welcome the two back, but unfortunately, without enough HATE left to keep their body whole, Chara begins to fade away.

They say their final goodbye and fully dust away, leaving their SOUL exposed. Together with the other six SOULs Asriel has, they merge into a poly-trait SOUL, acting as their final gift for him before leaving.

With the new SOUL, Asriel breaks the Barrier, freeing Monsterkind and letting them go to the Surface.<br>
In addition, he gives Frisk their SOUL back, allowing them to become physical and handing back the control over the SAVE file.

### Staying behind
Sans excuses him and Frisk the departure while the rest of the monsters leave and enter the human cities.

Sans remarks that this has been a one-of-a-kind ending, and none of this could have been possible without Frisk's actions.<br>
Contrary to this, he remembers the No Mercy runs that Frisk has conducted over the years, and he doesn't like that Hard Mode started all of this.<br>
He is afraid the glitches might come back to haunt them sooner or later, but until then, his focus is on Frisk.

Sans asks Frisk whether they are finally feeling satisfactory, and if they will let go of the RESET function.

Frisk promises they won't RESET anymore.

Sans isn't sure if they are telling the truth.

But he believes in what little trust they have built up.

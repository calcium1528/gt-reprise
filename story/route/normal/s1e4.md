## Plot (abridged)
Frisk wakes up in Snowed Inn while Sans and Flowey go over increasingly ridiculous plans on how to defeat Chara, two-thirds of which are suggested by Flowey.<br>
Sans crosses out half of Flowey's plans because they are literally impossible, which gets Flowey to complain. But he underlines one in particular twice.

Recalling the events before his latest RESET, Sans knows a person powerful enough who can help them stop Chara from messing with the timeline. Flowey is initially confused, but Sans says it will all make sense later.

Normally, Sans wouldn't have ever thought of doing something that ambitious. To bring the dead back—especially from a secluded place like the Void—already sounds crazy to most.<br>
But since Chara was able to return on their own, perhaps there is a slim chance he could do something.

The trio make their way to Waterfall, where somehow, against all odds, the Grey Room persisted after Sans's RESET. Both Sans and Flowey are visibly shocked, while Frisk doesn't have much of a reaction.<br>
Sans knocks on the grey door, and a response comes back from the other side, asking why the group came back.<br>
After Sans explains the current situation and why he is suitable, the door unlocks to reveal Gaster still exhausted from their last duel.

Gaster agrees to help the group, and he promises not to attack Frisk anymore. In return, Sans heals him and recovers his Chromatic Hands.

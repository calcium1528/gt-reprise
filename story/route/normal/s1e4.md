## Plot

### After the True Lab encounter
Frisk wakes up in Snowed Inn while Sans and Flowey go over increasingly ridiculous plans on how to defeat Chara, two-thirds of which are suggested by Flowey.<br>
Sans crosses out half of Flowey's plans because they are literally impossible, which gets Flowey to complain. But he underlines one in particular twice.

Recalling the events before his latest RESET, Sans knows a person powerful enough who can help them stop Chara from messing with the timeline. Flowey is initially confused, but Sans says it will all make sense later.

Normally, Sans wouldn't have ever thought of doing something that ambitious. To bring the dead back—especially from a secluded place like the Void—already sounds crazy to most.<br>
But since Chara was able to return on their own, perhaps there is a slim chance he could do something.

### Getting Gaster's help
The trio make their way to Waterfall, where somehow, against all odds, the Grey Room persisted after Sans's RESET. Both Sans and Flowey are visibly shocked, while Frisk doesn't have much of a reaction.<br>
Sans knocks on the grey door, and a response comes back from the other side, asking why the group came back.<br>
After Sans explains the current situation and why he is suitable, the door unlocks to reveal Gaster still exhausted from their last duel.

Gaster agrees to help the party, and he promises not to attack Frisk anymore. In return, Sans heals him and recovers his Chromatic Hands.

After a quick exchange, Gaster leads the party to the Throne Room, where the six prior fallen humans' SOULs are kept down the basement.<br>
Sans nudges Flowey to take the SOULs, in which he swiftly declines, but since it is the only real way to defeat Chara at the moment, he reconsiders and accepts the idea.<br>
The party then moves to the Barrier, where Flowey absorbs the SOULs and turns into his Omega form. This catches Chara's attention.

### Battle at the Barrier
Omega Flowey strikes first, leading the party's attack. Sans adds to his bullet patterns with bone barrages and blasters.<br>
Gaster shields Frisk with his Kindness hand while simultaneously supporting both fighters. Frisk watches from the sidelines.

Chara retaliates with an equally large amount of Determination knives that are coated with HATE, filling the area with dark red blades that actively try to restrain Sans and Omega Flowey.<br>
Both try to clear them off, but they regenerate too fast to be tackled properly, and a batch of tentacles is able to grip tight enough onto Flowey to restrict his movement considerably.

Once Chara has effectively immobilized Omega Flowey, they prepare to hit him directly. Gaster protects him with another Kindness hand, but Chara is able to shatter that shield and rip one of Flowey's arms clean off.

Another slash is prepared, this time targeting Sans. Before he could form a defense, Gaster moves another Chromatic Hand over, and that saves Sans's life at the cost of another disabled hand.

Omega Flowey, despite being weakened, still goes ahead to charge his ultimate attack—a giant beam of death—at Chara.<br>
Chara is seemingly blasted clean, but the attack wasn't enough to completely wipe them. Their vessel is half-vaoprized, yet they reform like all the damage never happened.<br>
Without any strength left, Omega Flowey isn't able to sustain his form any longer, and he reverts back to normal Flowey. The six SOULs are now free and able to be taken.

Chara tries to grab the SOULs for themself, but Frisk refuses to let that happen. As Chara reaches for one of them, Frisk's SOUL fills up with Determination, and they produce a wave of energy strong enough to knock them back.

Taking this sliver of time, Frisk gives their Determination SOUL to Flowey, and with the fight severely one-sided as of now, he takes it without a second thought.<br>
Together with the other six SOULs, he absorbs them and transforms to his God of Hyperdeath form, "ASRIEL DREEMURR".

Asriel greets Chara, saying he's their best friend.

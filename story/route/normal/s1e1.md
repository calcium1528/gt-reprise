# Megalomaniac
***Megalomaniac*** is the 1st episode of Season 1, otherwise abbreviated S1E1.

## Plot
Frisk is on another No Mercy run. After breezing through prior areas, they step foot into the Last Corridor, where they now confront Sans just like every other time.<br>
They SAVE once in the area in case they lose to Sans and have to respawn.

Sans recalls how this has ended for him every prior run. Whenever Frisk had gone down the No Mercy route, it had never resulted in anything good.<br>
He refers to it as "Monsterkind's practical extinction", and he doesn't believe Frisk will stop any time soon.

Frisk's first attempt at Sans doesn't go as expected. What they memorized was the correct way to dodge is now blocked by a wall of bones. They are unable to react fast enough and their HP quickly depletes to 0.<br>
Despite losing, Frisk proceeds to LOAD their SAVE file, which was conveniently last saved at the Last Corridor, putting them back to the beginning of the encounter.<br>
On the second attempt, Frisk falls for a Gaster Blaster trap, which gets them instantly killed since they were on low HP. Nevertheless, they LOAD again, and the encounter resumes.

Over the course of Frisk's multiple failed attempts, Sans ponders about what they truly want going down the No Mercy route, as he himself has no clue what fun is there in killing others.<br>
He isn't sure this is the Frisk he remembers. The Frisk he knew wasn't a murderer like the person he is seeing now. He can tell from Frisk's expression that they can't afford to care about what he says anymore, but he still advises them to RESET and leave his world alone.<br>
Another death is another attempt at the fight, and another reLOAD for Frisk. Minutes stretch to hours. Sans starts to grow TIRED.

Frisk dies again and again. Progress seems to have halted by the 7th attempt. They can't keep up with Sans's new and unpredictable bullet patterns. This was not part of their muscle memory.<br>
They begin to feel guilty of their actions. By the 8th attempt, they change strategies. If they can't win Sans using violence, they will gain his trust back. They try to SPARE Sans instead.

Frisk's actions get the attention of another individual. The first fallen child, Chara.<br>
Chara, having been Frisk's partner and followed them since the beginning, is not particularly happy about Frisk's sudden change of tactics. Being corrupted by Frisk's HATE—a dark substance manifesting from one's own hatred towards something, they can't support their sudden act to "abort the No Mercy run" when they have come so far.<br>
When Frisk eventually loses the will to fight, as they reach over to the RESET button, Chara traps them in a prison made of HATE and replaces Frisk as a SOULless vessel in the next LOAD.

Sans begins to struggle more and more as Chara continues to fight him and getting absurd near-misses without dying, much to Frisk's protest as they have to watch everything happen through the walls of the prison.<br>
With Sans worn down, Chara prepares the final strike to kill Sans. Frisk is able to break out of the prison just in time to block Chara's slash, becoming fatally wounded.<br>
Frisk apologizes to Sans for their past actions, and asks him to fix their mistakes. Their SOUL is left behind exposed. Before Chara could process what just happened and the SOUL could shatter, Sans swiftly grabs it and absorbs it, getting 2 ATK.

Sans's sudden power spike catches Chara off guard, and they are killed by a multitude of attacks shortly after.<br>
He performs an unprecedented monster-issued RESET at the Last Corridor, which becomes the first of many glitches that will begin plaguing the timeline from here on out.

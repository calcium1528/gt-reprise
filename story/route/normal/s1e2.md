## Plot
Frisk walks over to Sans's sentry station in Waterfall. They invite him to hang out at Grillby's. Sans leads them there using a shortcut he often takes.<br>
Oddly enough, while moving, Frisk ends up in a completely different location. It looks to be a hallway with a mysterious grey door.

Sans doesn't turn around and notice anything wrong, so he keeps going. Frisk isn't sure how they ended up here, but because they are curious, they open the door and walk in.

Entering the Grey Room, Gaster is waiting  for Frisk with a conveniently placed SAVE point beside him. The door instantly locks itself behind them.<br>
Inspecting the SAVE point, it reveals a completely unaltered file interface. That shouldn't exist in the Void, where timeline manipulation powers don't reliably function here.<br>

When Frisk reaches over to the RESET function, Gaster intervenes and takes temporary control over their file, restricting their access to both RESET and LOAD.<br>
Gaster goes over how by taking Frisk's SOUL, he could return to his home dimension and become whole again. He attacks Frisk first, overpowering them and capturing their SOUL.

Sans turns around and realizes Frisk has been missing from Grillby's. He leaves the place to later find the Grey Room, where the door to it is locked.<br>
Having a feeling Frisk might have just unknowingly walked up to here, Sans teleports himself inside the Grey Room.<br>
After moving in, Sans is unable to leave because the Void interferes with conventional magic. He uses a Gaster Blaster to blow the door wide open, letting him and Frisk flee.<br>
*(Casualties: 1 butterfly.)*

Gaster, now able to escape the Void, walks outside and returns to his original form, regaining his 7 Chromatic Hands.<br>
Sans knows he can't reasonably defeat Gaster in this form, so he asks Frisk for help again.

Just when Frisk hands out their SOUL to him, Gaster exploits that opportunity and intercepts the process to capture both Frisk and their SOUL, wrapping them in dark matter.<br>
Refusing to back down, Sans breaks the bubble with a Blaster, freeing the SOUL and letting him grab and absorb it.

A battle breaks out between Sans and Gaster. During the fight, Gaster rants about how bad he felt having been banished to the Void without any discernable reason, and why it had to be only him.<br>
He admits he just wanted to come back, but Sans notes what he tried to do in order to achieve that conflicts with his goal of protecting Frisk.

Sans is eventually able to tire Gaster out by disabling his Chromatic Hands one by one, weakening him over time. Eventually, Gaster can't keep up and stops attacking.

Gaster tries one more time to get Sans off-guard, but even his dark matter is starting to fade. Having no choice left, he recedes back to the Grey Room, and Sans performs another RESET.

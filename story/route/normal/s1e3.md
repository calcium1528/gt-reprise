# Determination
***Determination*** is the 3rd episode of Season 1, also written as S1E3.

## Featured

### Characters
- [Frisk](https://calcium1528.github.io/gt-reprise/character/frisk)
- [Chara](https://calcium1528.github.io/gt-reprise/character/chara)
- [Sans](https://calcium1528.github.io/gt-reprise/character/sans)
- [Flowey](https://calcium1528.github.io/gt-reprise/character/flowey)

### Miscellaneous
- HATE, because I refuse to call it a separate character. It is merely a living black blob of pure hatred.

## Plot
Frisk hangs out with Sans at the hot dog stand in Hotland.<br>
Sans starts talking about how they should not RESET anymore, saying it can have side effects such as creating timeline anomalies and events that are not supposed to happen.<br>
Meanwhile, a shadowy figure is lurking in the True Lab, waiting for the eventual arrival of their partner.

A dark substance is leaking out from the corners of the True Lab elevator's door. Sans notices first and warns Frisk not to enter, but curiosity wins out and they still go anyway.<br>
The ghostly figure turns out to be Chara.<br>
They bear a empty Determination SOUL, made from the accumulating glitches the timeline has been experiencing. Their vessel is just tangible enough to stand without collapsing, being composed of the same dark substance that Frisk found earlier: HATE.

Right next to Chara is the Determination Extraction machine. Chara blames Frisk for the countless No Mercy runs they have done, building up HATE with each subsequent run until that substance made its way into Chara, saturating them and corrupting their mind.<br>
Chara explains their plan directly to Frisk: they want to take their Determination by any means neccesary and erase this timeline through their powers.<br>
Before Frisk could do anything to protest, Chara uses the dark substance to trap them in the Extraction machine. The machine then automatically starts operating.

Flowey, watching from a "conveinently placed chunk of transparent concrete" behind Chara, now leaves to inform Sans—who is currently in Snowdin—of Chara's return.<br>
Sans initially shrugs it off, but after Flowey goes into Frisk's state, well, he knows he's going to have a bad day.

Arriving at the True Lab, Sans and Flowey confront Chara directly, stating they are here to save Frisk. Chara ignores them and engages first.<br>
They exchange blows while the Extraction machine keeps running in the background. Attempts to stop it fail because the dark substance is encasing it and deflecting all incoming attacks. When any attacks do connect, the substance heals to quickly for that damage to matter.

Eventually, the Extraction machine finishes, and its Determination reservoir opens up. Sans, Flowey, and Chara race to take it, with Flowey trying to restrain Chara so Sans can reach it in time.<br>
Chara slices themself in half to free themself. Flowey can't regrab them quick enough, and they fly straight for the Determination.<br>
The Determination flows into their SOUL immediately, and a shockwave follows right after, covering the area in smoke.

The Extraction machine later opens up and Frisk falls onto the ground unconscious. Sans and Flowey retreat with a limp Frisk.<br>
When the dust settles, Chara now has Frisk's timeline manipulation abilities, and uses them to overwrite the name in their SAVE file with theirs.

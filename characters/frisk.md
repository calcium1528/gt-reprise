So their lore is complicated. Real complicated. Got me utterly devastated at how much we know yet simultaneously don't know about them and their powers.

The first flaw, and I think this is the only MAJOR gripe I have with canon, is their gender.<br>
How dare you gender Frisk. How. Why. What was the reason? I don't see any valid cause.<br>
Maybe it's just me. I don't know. I don't care. For the purposes of Reprise, Frisk is specifically nonbinary.

2nd is their age. More specifically, how they DON'T age.<br>
Canon says they were around the age of 14 when they fell into the Underground, and they have continuously reset for 30 YEARS STRAIGHT to explore every possible outcome resulting from their actions, including every possible Neutral ending. Okay, maybe the insane time span has some merit to it.<br>
It would have been fine if they had also aged appropriately, but canon simply said "eh, screw you" and threw the entire concept of "growing up" into the nearest dumpster fire.<br>
Part of it survived the fire hazard, in the sense that they still mentally aged, but not physically. That to me is still an issue with the lore.

3rd is their development. Well, stat development.<br>
Canon says they have completed maybe a handful of No Mercy runs. In all of those runs, Frisk never finished a single one and never once reached LV 20 as "there was no previous LV 20 data", which prevented them from ever getting past LV 19.<br>
An obvious question arises: how did they even get LV 19 in the first place?<br>
Who performed the first No Mercy route? Who slimed the Underground first, then RESET because there was nothing else meaningful to do, and be mysteriously replaced by a nonbinary child with a stick and unbridled levels of determination?<br>
I don't like answering that. So let's ignore it entirely.

4th are their relationships, notably with Chara and Sans.<br>
I get that they don't necessarily have good relations with Chara, especially when you consider the fact Frisk was the one to perform all the No Mercy runs and generate HATE from their hatred towards monsters, which later went on to corrupt Chara. That's perfectly fine.<br>
As with Sans, I'm gonna say real quick that he's paranoid. For once the human actually did something good, reviving Gaster, bringing back Asriel, freeing monsterkind and all, but the thing he fears most isn't even the essence of fear itself. It's not Betty or her illusion tricks. It's the damn RESET button.<br>
His instant severance of friendship with Frisk is somewhat justified. There's no guarantee the same sequence of events that led here will ever happen again. The RESET button therefore is the equivalent of a nuclear bomb that can wipe entire timelines.  Except the freaking button has already been destroyed! So you can't even worry about some random kid accidentally erasing your entire existence because they physically can't!

Expect me to rant more later on when this site grows.
